## Responsibilities

Maintainers are responsible for:

- Reviewing and merging PRs
- Enforcing the axiom policy
- Ensuring CI/CD systems are functioning
- Facilitating discussions and decisions
- Publishing releases
- Maintaining the index and documentation

## Becoming a Maintainer

We welcome new maintainers! The process:

1. Active contributions over time
2. Understanding of the project goals and policies
3. Nomination by existing maintainer or self-nomination
4. Consensus among current maintainers

## Decision Making

- Technical decisions: consensus among maintainers
- Breaking changes: require explicit approval announcement
- Axiom additions: follow the review contract in [`docs/2_ARCHITECTURE.md`](../docs/2_ARCHITECTURE.md)
- Emergency decisions: majority vote with documentation

## Contact

For maintainer-specific issues, please open an issue with the `maintainer` label.
