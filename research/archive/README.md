# Research and Documentation Archive

This directory preserves source transcripts, historical reports, detailed specifications, and superseded policies used during the August 17, 2026 documentation consolidation.

Archived files are retained for provenance. They are not current project policy or status, and their links, claims, affiliations, and build results may be stale. Use the five documents under [`docs/`](../../docs/) for current guidance.

## Contents

- Root-level files preserve research transcripts and former project-specific strategy documents.
- `governance/` preserves the former technical policies and full project specification now consolidated into `docs/2_ARCHITECTURE.md`.
- `guidance/` preserves domain-specific implementation notes distilled into the architecture and spectral-theory pillars.
- `status/` preserves mutually inconsistent historical QA and build snapshots superseded by the generated scoreboard.
